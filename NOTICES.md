# Sources, software, and acknowledgements

## Film and artwork

The experience is inspired by *The Book of Life* (2014), directed by Jorge R. Gutiérrez and produced by Reel FX / 20th Century Fox. It is an independent educational fan project, not an official release or endorsed website.

Film imagery and production art belong to their respective rights holders. No open reuse permission has been verified. Full acquisition details are in `ASSET-SOURCES.md` and `asset-manifest.json`.

- Film reference: https://family.20thcenturystudios.com/movies/the-book-of-life
- Rendered city panorama and production feature: https://www.sidefx.com/community/the-book-of-life/
- Story stills: https://www.fandango.com/the-book-of-life-163450/movie-photos-posters
- Character and family images: https://www.sensacine.com/peliculas/pelicula-203878/fotos/
- Gate design reference, Jordan Lamarre-Wan: https://www.theartofjlw.com/book-of-life
- Painted concept-art reference collection: https://characterdesignreferences.com/art-of-animation-1/art-of-the-book-of-life

The concept-art sheets remain research references and are not presented as final rendered scene illustrations. Embedded source credits are preserved in the reference originals.

The entrance gate uses [Clipart Library's Gothic Cemetery Gate image](https://clipart-library.com/clipart/cemetery-cliparts-22.htm), listed by the publisher for personal use and school projects. Its baked checkerboard was removed using the built-in image editing tool, and the prepared derivative is layered in CSS. It is not official film gate artwork. See `GATE-ASSET.md` for the source and full preparation prompt.

## Original website material

The website's narrative captions, discovery text, decorative controls, CSS book artifact, procedural Canvas effects, six-second atmospheric video, and synthesized ambient audio were produced for this implementation. The entrance gate's underlying artwork is sourced as credited above. The ambient audio is not the film soundtrack. Design and code credit can be assigned to the real project team in `dist/model/project-info.json`.

The brief and approved seven-spread outline supplied by the project owner guided the experience.
